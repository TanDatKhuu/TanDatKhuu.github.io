About me

I am an Applied Mathematics graduate from Ton Duc Thang University, Vietnam. My main research interests lie in nonlinear partial differential equations, especially fully nonlinear elliptic equations, regularity theory, and mathematical analysis.

My recent work focuses on Harnack inequalities, Liouville-type rigidity, and principal eigenvalues for singular and degenerate fully nonlinear elliptic equations with Hamiltonian terms. I have also worked on infinity elliptic equations and regularity estimates for non-uniformly elliptic problems.

I am interested in broadening my mathematical background toward mathematical physics and related problems. Alongside research, I teach mathematics part-time and enjoy participating in seminars, summer schools, and research discussions.
