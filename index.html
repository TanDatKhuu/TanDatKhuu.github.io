About me

I am an Applied Mathematics graduate from Ton Duc Thang University, Vietnam. My main research interests lie in nonlinear partial differential equations, especially fully nonlinear elliptic equations, regularity theory, and mathematical analysis.

My recent work focuses on Harnack inequalities, Liouville-type rigidity, and principal eigenvalues for singular and degenerate fully nonlinear elliptic equations with Hamiltonian terms. I have also worked on infinity elliptic equations and regularity estimates for non-uniformly elliptic problems.

I am interested in broadening my mathematical background toward mathematical physics and related problems. Alongside research, I teach mathematics part-time and enjoy participating in seminars, summer schools, and research discussions.

Research

Selected research projects, beginning with my more recent work in nonlinear elliptic equations.

Harnack Theory and Rigidity for Singular and Degenerate Fully Nonlinear Elliptic Equations with Hamiltonians

Dec 2025 – Aug 2026

Global C¹,γ regularity, Harnack inequalities, Liouville-type rigidity, and principal eigenvalues for nonlinear elliptic equations with Hamiltonian terms.

Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Hoang-Hung Vo · International University, VNU–HCM

Liouville-Type Results for Infinity Elliptic Equations Involving Gradient and Hardy–Hénon Nonlinearities

Aug 2025 – Mar 2026

Weighted comparison principles, existence and uniqueness, radial analysis, and Liouville-type properties for infinity elliptic equations.

Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Hoang-Hung Vo · International University, VNU–HCM

Regularity for Solutions to Non-Uniformly Elliptic Equations in Generalized Function Spaces

Aug 2024 – Jul 2025

Bachelor's thesis on global gradient estimates for double-phase equations with (p, q)-growth in Lorentz, Orlicz, and generalized Morrey-type spaces.

Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Minh-Phuong Tran · Ton Duc Thang University

Multistep Methods and Numerical Simulations for Real-World Models

Aug 2024 – Jul 2025

Adams–Bashforth and Adams–Moulton methods: convergence, consistency, stability, and numerical experiments in Python.

Supervisors: Assoc. Prof. Minh-Phuong Tran and Dr. Huu-Can Nguyen · TDTU

Existence Results for Semilinear Fractional Elliptic Equations

Jun – Aug 2024

Nonnegative weak solutions, fractional Laplacians, Green operator estimates, and fixed-point arguments.

Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Phuoc-Tai Nguyen · Masaryk University

Regularity for Non-Uniformly Elliptic Double Obstacle Problems

Jun 2023 – Oct 2024

Fractional maximal operators, good-λ inequalities, and global estimates in Lorentz and Orlicz–Sobolev spaces.

Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Minh-Phuong Tran · Ton Duc Thang University
